    {{> systems/dcc/templates/actor-partial-equipment-ammunition.html }} {{!-- WF BLOCK 1: no armor section (REQ-SHEET-014). Weird Frontiers has no MUNDANE armor: no armor table, no armor price list, nothing to buy or equip. Protection comes from COVER instead, adjudicated by the judge per situation and not tracked on the sheet: Partial +1, Half +2, Full +4 (Weird_Frontiers_V6.md:2974-2978). The AC rule states the unarmored case as the general one, with no armored alternative anywhere (:2852). The word "armor" DOES appear in both books, and none of it is an inventory item: - magical and contraption armor — Golemite Armor (:5065), the Armor of Darkness spell (:7784) — grants a TEMPORARY AC bonus for a duration. That is an Active Effect, not an armor item with a static acBonus; - three melee weapons grant "+1d ... versus armored opponents" (Katar, Mace, Pick — Weird_Frontiers_Reference_Guide.md:1394, :1398, :1402). That is about NPCs being armored, not about player characters wearing armor; - monsters have natural armor in their stat blocks (e.g. chitin, :13637). The `armor` ITEM TYPE is deliberately left registered — this module can be installed in a world that also runs plain DCC characters, and removing a type from the system would break them. DCC's AC computation still sums equipped armor (reference/dcc/module/actor.js:226-243); with no way to add armor from this sheet it simply sums nothing. Ammunition is kept: WF firearms very much use it. --}}
